# Manifestation apps on Google Play: competitive research

*Researched 18 Oct 2026. Covers what a Gen Z user sees after searching "manifest" on Google Play.*

## How this was researched (read first)

The research tools could not open Google Play, AppBrain or most tracker pages directly (DNS blocked), so the figures below come from **search-result snippets of Play listings, third-party trackers (App Pricing Lab, Grand Screen, AppFollow, AppGoblin), teardown sites (Screensdesign), review summarisers (Kimola, Justuseapp, marlvel.ai), press releases and journalist reviews (Bustle)**. Treat install tiers and ratings as snapshots that are close but not exact. Where a number is iOS-only or self-reported, it says so. Before quoting any number publicly, check it on the live Play listing on a phone.

## 1. The apps

### Big, general affirmation apps (the ones with the downloads)

**1. I am – Daily affirmations** (Monkey Taps LLC, `com.hrd.iam`)
- **What it does:** a feed of affirmation cards you swipe through, with categories, backgrounds, reminders all day, a home-screen widget, a journal and Wear OS. It's text-first and has no real audio loop.
- **Installs/rating:** about **10M+ installs, 4.8★ from ~302K Play ratings** ([App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/app/google_play/com.hrd.iam), [Grand Screen](https://grand-screen.com/apps/i-am-daily-affirmations/)).
- **Money:** free with **ads** plus subscriptions. iOS lists 10 IAPs from $9.99 up to **$149.99 lifetime**. AppGoblin estimates about **69% of revenue comes from ads** ([AppGoblin](https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/874656917)).
- **Complaints:** a premium pop-up every time the app opens, and a close button that doesn't work; being charged more than once after switching plans; the streak counter glitching after a few weeks; affirmations that get repetitive; free-tier ads "can get annoying" ([unitQ](https://unitq.com/unitq-scorecards/iampositiveaffirmations), [Grand Screen](https://grand-screen.com/apps/i-am-daily-affirmations/)). A Google Play review summary for a similarly named "I am: Positive Affirmations" mentions a **robotic voice feature** and notification bugs ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-from-i-am-positive-affirmations-user-feedback-google-play-157600)).

**2. Motivation – Daily quotes** (Monkey Taps): the sister app to I am, built around widgets and quotes. One tracker says 32M+ downloads and 4.8★; another says far less, so the true number is unclear ([Spark/MWM](https://spark.mwm.ai/ja/apps/motivation-daily-quotes/876080126), [Screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/motivation-daily-quotes)). It matters because it shows how much **widgets** drive daily use in this category.

**3. Moonly: Moon Phases & Calendar** (Cosmic Vibrations Inc)
- **What it does:** moon calendar, astrology, rituals, tarot, morning affirmations, and new-moon manifesting.
- **Installs/rating:** about **3.3M installs (1M+ tier), 4.1★** ([Grand Screen](https://grand-screen.com/apps/moonly-moon-phases-calendar/)).
- **Money:** ads plus many tiers ($3.99/mo, $29.99–$49.99/yr, $39.99 lifetime, weekly plans) ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/moonly-astrology-app-review), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/US/app/id1489889871)).
- **Complaints:** annoying ads unless you pay, a lot locked behind the paywall, some features only on iOS, and the calendar sometimes showing the wrong day. It is the closest rival on **mystical theming**, but it is an astrology app, not an affirmations app.

### Manifestation-specific apps

**4. Manifest: Vision Board Journal / "Manifestation Journal"** (Just Imagine Studio, `justimaginestudio.com.mindset_achieveyourgoals`)
- **What it does:** goal tracker, vision board, daily affirmations, reflections, a manifestation journal, visualisation, and subliminal audio.
- **Installs/rating:** **1M+ installs, 4.6★ from ~7.2K ratings** (from a Play listing snippet; [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=justimaginestudio.com.mindset_achieveyourgoals&hl=en_US)). It is probably the biggest pure "manifest" app on Play.
- **Money:** freemium with IAP. **Complaints:** no reliable review data was found. It's a typical everything-in-one app, broad but shallow.

**5. Innertune Affirmations** (Innertune Media)
- **What it does:** a quiz builds a **21-day audio program**. It offers 10 voices, record-your-own, background beats, playlists and reminders. In Dec 2025 it added AI-built programs drawn from 22,000 pre-recorded affirmations ([press release](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/abnewswire-2025-12-1-innertune-affirmations-reaches-12-million-users-launches-ai-powered-affirmation-program-personalization), [Screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/innertune-affirmations)).
- **Scale:** it claims **1.2M users and 4.9★ across 32K reviews** (App Store and Play combined, self-reported). Revenue is about $70K/month per [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/innertune-a-70k-month-audio-affirmations-app-Psng8dtcMtX2c8oCzGAz). The Android listing couldn't be confirmed independently.
- **Money:** the paywall has monthly and annual plans **with no free trial**. Closing it brings up a **60%-off countdown pop-up**.
- **Complaints:** **saved playlists disappearing within 24–48 hours** (one user re-recorded 32 affirmations three times); the monthly price feels too high for how slowly the app improves; worries about data sharing ([Justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6443625379/innertune-daily-affirmations/reviews)).
- **Note:** it is the **closest rival to our audio approach**, but its "AI" picks from pre-recorded lines. It doesn't write affirmations about *your* situation.

**6. ThinkUp – Daily Affirmations** (Precise Wellness, `com.think.up`)
- **What it does:** you record affirmations in your own voice and loop them over music. It has a practice timer and reminders.
- **Rating:** 4.7★ from ~7.5K on iOS. Play numbers weren't found.
- **Money:** **$39.99/yr, $7.99/mo, $99.99 lifetime**. The free tier allows only 3 recordings and 1 music track ([AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/thinkup-daily-affirmations-app/906660772?country=au), [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/manifest-out-loud-thinkup-app-review)).
- **Complaints (Play):** notifications that don't arrive, **recordings you can't get back**, slow support ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/thinkup-app-review-analysis-insights-for-growth-google-play-157588)).
- **Note:** it charges **the same yearly price as our planned $39.99**, so it's the clearest price comparison.

**7. Aya: Manifest Your Dream Self** (Lit Apps LLC): **100K+ installs, 4.6★ from ~20.2K Play reviews**, IAP ([Play snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/eventdetails/4830300594040628894), [Screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/aya-manifest-your-dream-self/)). It's one of the fastest-rated newer "dream self" apps. Few review details were found.

**8. Stella – Manifest Anything**: AI writes a **personalised audio "story"** from your goals, which you can edit by prompt. It also has gratitude and swipe-to-save affirmations. ## 3. The most common flaws across the category (most common first)

1. **Aggressive or deceptive paywalls.** Hard paywalls straight after a long, personal quiz (Stella); no trial plus a 60%-off countdown (Innertune); a premium pop-up on every launch (I am); weekly prices you only see after 10 minutes of setup (Stella). One reviewer says users "hit a paywall within minutes and abandon the search" ([LoA blog](https://www.loa-lawofattraction.co/blog/best-free-manifestation-apps-2026)). A Play review surfaced in 2026 app roundups describes loving a widget app's quiz, then finding the affirmation stopped changing until they subscribed (exact app not confirmed).
2. **Ads in a "calm" app.** I am (about 69% of revenue from ads) and Moonly both get "ads are annoying" reviews. Ads break the mood the product is selling.
3. **Generic, repetitive content.** "10,000 affirmations" libraries (I am, App Origins) feel repetitive within weeks ([Grand Screen](https://grand-screen.com/apps/i-am-daily-affirmations/)). Even Innertune's "AI" picks from fixed lines.
4. **Lost data and saves that don't stick.** Innertune playlists vanish; ThinkUp recordings can't be opened; journals across this space lose entries after updates ([Justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6443625379/innertune-daily-affirmations/reviews), [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/thinkup-app-review-analysis-insights-for-growth-google-play-157588), [marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/daynote-diary-with-password/reviews)).
5. **Broken reminders and streaks.** These are the core habit loop, and they're the most reported bugs (I am streak glitch; ThinkUp and I am notification problems).
6. **Robotic or bad voices.** TTS voices are called robotic (I am), and system TTS "may mispronounce words" ([AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/affirmation-ss/1036700072?country=us)).
7. **Billing surprises.** Double charges (I am), emailing support just to cancel (Innertune), and a confusing mix of weekly, monthly and lifetime plans (Moonly).
8. **Android is treated as second-class.** Features are iOS-only (Moonly), and the best newer apps are iOS-only (Brilla, VibeSesh, Myla, Stella).
9. **Promising too much.** Store copy like "results in 7 days" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6753663282)) is a trust and policy risk. CoddyKit is the only app that openly says it can't guarantee results.

## 4. What our app already does better

- **Real personalisation.** Claude writes affirmations from *your own* description of what's "already yours". Competitors either use a fixed library (I am, Innertune) or are iOS-only (Stella). This fixes flaw 3 directly.
- **Better writing.** The mix of "you" and "I", the "why" questions and the "It's done" closers is more varied and more psychologically interesting than flat "I am…" lists.
- **Natural voices.** ElevenLabs answers the "robotic voice" complaint (flaw 6). Only Innertune has studio-quality voices, and it doesn't use them for personalised scripts.
- **One audio studio.** Spoken *or* subliminal, 1–2× speed, ocean/rain/birds/pads, 528 Hz frequencies, a mixer and your own voice, all in one place. On Play this is currently split across Manifestar (subliminal/theta), ThinkUp (own voice) and Innertune (voices).
- **Ready-made tracks** (money, confidence, love, luck, glow up, sleep) match Gen Z searches, so first use takes zero effort.
- **Mystical themes** (celestial, cosmic aura, tarot) look like TikTok manifestation content. Most rivals use pastel stock photos.
- **An honest paywall** ($39.99/yr with a 7-day trial, no dark patterns). It matches ThinkUp's price, but with far more features and without the pressure tactics Innertune and Stella use.
- **Android-first.** We're building for the platform the best newer apps ignore.

## 5. Opportunities, ranked by impact for a Gen Z audience

1. **A home-screen and lock-screen widget showing *your* affirmation.** Widgets are why the biggest apps (I am, Motivation) get used daily, and Gen Z shares home screens on TikTok. Ours would show Claude-written lines in the user's theme, change through the day, and **stay free**. This answers the "widget stopped changing until I paid" complaint. For the PWA, start with a daily notification and a "set as wallpaper" image export; build a real Android widget once we go native (Capacitor/TWA).
2. **AI 369 method.** 369 is the best-known TikTok method, but on Play it only exists as text trackers (Manifesty, CoddyKit). Ours: Claude turns your desire into one short 369 sentence; you write or tap it 3, 6 and 9 times with morning, afternoon and night reminders, over 33 or 45 days; then a "listen to it" audio version plays in your voice or an ElevenLabs voice. This pulls in the "369 manifestation" searches.
3. **Scripting journal plus an evidence journal ("It's done" log).** Scripting means writing a diary entry from the future as if it already happened. Claude can turn a scripting entry into a matching affirmation track. An **evidence or "signs" log** ("what showed up today") closes the loop and gives users something to share. It needs **reliable saving** with export and backup, because lost data is flaw 4. This fits our "already yours" idea and our tone.
4. **Shareable "aura cards" and an AI vision board.** Turn a track or desire into a themed card (celestial or tarot style) for Stories and TikTok, with a "made with [app]" mark. Add a light vision board that uses your own photos plus generated card art. Vision boards are a top search term, and every rival's version looks dated. This is our cheapest marketing.
5. **Sleep mode / overnight subliminal session.** Use what we already have: a sleep timer, fade-out, screen-off playback, theta or 528 Hz under whispered personalised lines. Manifestar is the only Android competitor and it's small. "Manifest while you sleep" is a high-intent search.
6. **Reminders and streaks that actually work, with streak protection.** It sounds boring, but broken reminders and streaks are the most common bug in the category. Add one "freeze" day so users don't quit when they break a streak.
7. **Moon-phase rituals as a light extra.** Add new- and full-moon prompts to the daily tip. That takes some of Moonly's 3M-user audience without building astrology.
8. **Light community later** (anonymous "it's done" wins wall). No competitor does community well. It's high effort and needs moderation, so leave it until after launch.

Smaller items: a "methods" library (55x5, whisper, pillow, mirror work) as short guided cards; cloud backup on signup; volume ducking so tracks can play over music.

## 6. Positioning and store-listing angle

**The angle:** *"Affirmations written about your life, read to you in a real voice."* Every other result for "manifest" is a generic library or a text tracker. We should lead with **personal + natural voice + subliminal**, and use the mystical look to stand out visually.

- **Title (30 chars):** something like **"[Name]: Manifest & Affirmations"**. Put "Manifest" and "Affirmations" early, since Play ranks the title most heavily.
- **Short description (80 chars):** e.g. *"Your affirmations, written for you and read in a real voice. Subliminal + 369."*
- **First screenshot:** a typed desire ("I got the apartment by the sea") turning into a written track with a waveform, captioned *"Written for you. Not from a list."*
- **Following screenshots:** natural voices / your own voice; subliminal + 528 Hz mixer; 369 and scripting; widget; themes (celestial, aura, tarot).
- **Long description keywords:** manifest, manifestation, law of attraction, law of assumption, 369 method, scripting, subliminal, affirmations, lucky girl, glow up, sleep affirmations, 528 Hz, vision board (only once we have it).
- **Trust signals that target rivals' 1★ reviews:** "No ads. Ever." · "Free trial, cancel in two taps." · "Your affirmations are saved and backed up." · "Your words stay private." Each one answers a recurring complaint.
- **Be honest about results.** Present it as a mindset and self-talk tool (like CoddyKit's disclaimer). Avoid "results in 7 days". It protects the listing from policy issues and fits the honest-paywall brand.
- **Why the timing works:** in 2022, 70% of 13–39-year-olds agreed "you can manifest what you want" ([YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2022/03/18/manifesting-is-gen-z-and-millennials-new-take-on-spirituality/)). #manifestation passed 34.6 billion TikTok views by 2023 ([BPS](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/manifestation-believe-and-achieve)). 43% of Gen Z say they've relied on manifestation for money ([BadCredit.org](https://www.badcredit.org/studies/survey-manifestation-led-by-gen-z/)). The demand is there, and on Android it's being served by ad-heavy card apps.
