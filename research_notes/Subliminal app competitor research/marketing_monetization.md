# Marketing, Monetization and Audience for Subliminal / Manifestation Apps

Researched 2026-11-14. Many figures are third-party estimates or vendor blogs; each is labelled with its year and source. Disk on the research PC was nearly full, so no raw files were saved; everything below came from web search and page fetches.

## 1. The YouTube and TikTok subliminal creator scene

### Takeaway
Subliminals are a large, mostly faceless, young-female, beauty-led YouTube niche. The biggest channels are in the low hundreds of thousands of subscribers (not millions). Creators make money mainly through Patreon tiers and side stores selling "premium" subliminals; I found no confirmed creator-launched or creator-partnered app.

### Cited Findings
- Subscriber counts from a Feb 2026 roundup (secondary, not Social Blade): Eggtopia 412K (beauty, sleep, skin, health, money, weight loss); Kapelsu 369K (skin, hair growth, money, appearance); Beauty Krystalized 238K (manifestation, law of attraction); Solar Subs 200K; Akuo Subliminals 187K (subliminals plus ASMR, Germany); Moza Morph 184K (affirmations over rain/fire/music); HypnoDaddy 178K (long sleep/study audio); Luminalplay (count not given; beauty, confidence, academics, younger audience, sells a "One Day Formula") — [ytshark, Feb 2026](https://ytshark.com/?p=166)
- Older data point: Kapelsu had 286K subs and 106M+ views from 80+ uploads in 2022 — [TubeRanker (2022)](https://tuberanker.com/blog/best-subliminal-youtube-channels). Growth to 369K by 2026 suggests slow growth for a top channel.
- Rose Subliminals and Emperor Subliminals: no reliable current counts found (see Gaps).
- Format: community calls them "subs"; videos run minutes to hours, one image or looped animation over music; comment sections indicate a largely Gen Z audience; core promise is cosmetic perfection "without surgery or injections" — [Snake Oil substack](https://snakeoildotbiz.substack.com/p/what-the-hell-are-internet-subliminals); [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-blind-faith-of-subliminal-tiktok-videos)
- Nov 2025: subliminals "still pervasive" on YouTube; creators described as young, female and usually faceless; example titles "princess aeolian beautification", "ANGELMAXXING", "Play this once. The next 3 days will feel like a dream."; audio mixes static with Lana Del Rey fragments; surfaced via recommendations after watching women's self-confidence videos — [New Statesman, Nov 2025](https://www.newstatesman.com/culture/life/2025/11/youtube-subliminals-want-to-make-you-perfect)
- TikTok "results" videos travel: a "My amazing subliminal results" TikTok had 1.4M views — [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-blind-faith-of-subliminal-tiktok-videos)
- Monetization: LUMINALPLAY (est. 2017) runs a Patreon with 96 paid members at the time indexed — [Patreon/luminalplay](https://www.patreon.com/luminalplay). Iceyx's Patreon points to a separate Payhip store for premium subliminals — [Patreon/Iceyx73](https://www.patreon.com/Iceyx73). Nightshade's Auralcraft keeps subliminals free and uses Patreon as a supporter tier — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/nightshadesauralcraft/about). "I want it, I got it" has posted 400+ listener "results" pictures/videos as social proof — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/iwantitigotit). Absolute Power Subliminals sells "boosters" — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/ApoldSubliminals)
- Adjacent creator-to-app example: a creator whose affirmation audio clip got 12M+ views launched a "Resonance" app (free affirmations, breathwork, sound healing); no download/revenue figures — [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/personal-growth/video/have-you-ever-seen-this-trend-0929d3a3-bdba-4a69-9409-1652f0e43b48/) (thin source)

### Inferences
- Patreon member counts look small (tens to low hundreds), so most creators earn modestly. An app offering a revenue share, a creator "store" inside the app, or affiliate codes could be attractive to mid-size creators (50K–400K subs) who are faceless and under-monetized.
- The best-performing themes for the audience are appearance (desired face, skin, hair, "glow up"), then money, confidence/academics and sleep. "Results" testimonials are the core persuasion format; that maps directly onto TikTok slideshow/POV content.
- Because the leaders are only ~200–400K subs, partnering with several mid-tier channels is realistic for a solo founder in a way that big-wellness influencer deals are not.

### Gaps
- No Social Blade data could be pulled (not attempted due to tool budget); Rose Subliminals and Emperor Subliminals counts not verified.
- No price data on custom/commissioned subliminals found.
- No confirmed case of a major subliminal YouTuber launching or partnering with an app.
- #subliminal TikTok hashtag view totals not found (TikTok Creative Center would be needed).

## 2. How manifestation / affirmation apps market

### Takeaway
Consumer self-improvement apps in 2025–2026 grow mainly through TikTok organic slideshows run across many faceless or affiliate accounts, plus paid Apple Search Ads for intent keywords. Specific public case studies for affirmation/subliminal apps are scarce; the evidence comes from adjacent apps and vendor blogs.

### Cited Findings
- Slideshow playbooks (vendor blogs, self-reported): Stronger Mobile (gym planner) grew via tension hooks plus founder selfies, framed as 700K users in 275 days — [Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/the-slideshow-strategy-that-generated-700000-users-in-275-days); an astrology app gets 50M+ views/month from multi-account, multi-language slideshows — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/tiktok-multi-account-slideshow-strategy-making-50m-views-per-month/); an app with 100 affiliate accounts and 1B+ TikTok views — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/this-app-has-100-affiliate-accounts-and-more-than-1bn-views-on-tiktok); Cal AI repeatedly doubles down on slideshow formats that spike downloads — [Stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/cal-ai-tiktok-marketing-playbook)
- Self-growth app Astra: 5M views and 25K downloads from a single UGC video — [Sideshift case study](https://www.sideshift.app/casestudies/astra)
- Market leader "I am – Daily Affirmations" (Monkey Taps): estimated $400K/month revenue and 300K downloads in Feb 2021 — [Sensor Tower (2021)](https://sensortower.com/ios/us/monkey-taps/app/i-am-positive-affirmations/874656917/); later estimates $550–700K/month — [Screens Design](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/i-am-daily-affirmations), [Paywall Screens](https://www.paywallscreens.com/apps/i-am-daily-affirmations-mobile-paywall-5518) (estimates conflict); 10M+ Google Play downloads — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hrd.iam&hl=en_US). Uses a long onboarding quiz ending in a paywall, 3-day free trial into a yearly plan shown as a monthly equivalent, and cross-promotes the developer's other apps mid-onboarding — [Screens Design](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/i-am-daily-affirmations)
- Existing subliminal apps are small: VibeSesh – Subliminal Maker ~60K installs, 8.9K MAU, est. <$10K/month, 100% IAP — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6746336493); Manifest Money – Subliminal (Android) ~58K installs, 50/50 IAP and ads — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.exceptionalapps.total_money_reprogram); Subliminal: Manifest Goals 25.6K installs — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/531981452/keywords). Other entrants: Fate – Subliminals, Whispurry, SoulSync — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/fate-subliminals/6744332634), [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6753062688), [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6754273471)
- Apple Search Ads, Health & Fitness, US (2026 report): ~$1.59 cost per tap, ~$3.83 CPI; all-category median CPI $1.80 — [AppTweak 2026](https://apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-benchmarks) (same figures as its 2025 edition, possibly carried over). Another source: H&F avg CPT $2.80, CPI $7.50, 37% conversion — [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/?p=5305). Category tables also in [SplitMetrics 2026](https://splitmetrics.com/apple-ads-search-results-benchmarks-2026/benchmarks-by-app-categories) (not read).

### Inferences
- The "subliminal" app keyword space is filled with small apps (<$10K/month), which means search intent exists but nobody dominates it; ASO for "subliminal", "subliminal maker", "custom subliminal" should be winnable. "Affirmations" is dominated by I am, so compete on the subliminal angle.
- With H&F RPI ~$0.48 at Day 14 (section 4) and Apple Ads CPI of $3.8–7.5, paid ads will lose money early on unless conversion is well above median. Organic TikTok (slideshows of "results", "POV: you listened to your desired face subliminal for 30 days", making your own subliminal in-app) plus mid-tier subliminal creator affiliates is the realistic launch channel; Apple Search Ads only on exact, cheap "subliminal" keywords.
- Multi-account slideshow strategies are copied widely; the in-app "make your own subliminal" flow is a natural screen-recording format.

### Gaps
- No public case study of a subliminal or affirmation app's TikTok campaign with real numbers.
- Google App Campaign / YouTube ads CPIs for this niche not found.
- No keyword volume data for "subliminal", "369", "manifest" (needs AppTweak/Appfigures access).

## 3. Audience and trends

### Takeaway
The audience skews young and female (Gen Z and young millennials), in the US/UK/Australia plus other English-speaking markets. Manifestation interest spiked in 2020 and stayed high through 2025; lucky girl syndrome was a 2023 peak. Hard 2026 data is thin.

### Cited Findings
- 2022 survey of 2,300 Americans: 52% believe in manifestation, 28% actively practice it — [Shane Co survey via WWD](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-trends/zappos-shoeperstitions-report-questions-tiktok-trends-lucky-girl-syndrome-shoe-theory-spring-manifestation-gen-z-millennials-1238813353/)
- Zappos March 2023 survey (2,000 Americans): millennials, not Gen Z, ranked most likely to be influenced by TikTok manifestation trends — [WWD (2023)](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-trends/zappos-shoeperstitions-report-questions-tiktok-trends-lucky-girl-syndrome-shoe-theory-spring-manifestation-gen-z-millennials-1238813353/)
- Lucky girl syndrome took off at the end of Dec 2022 (Laura Galebe video), aimed at people in their 20s; hashtag view figures vary widely by source (149.6M to 1.3B+, undated) — [Deseret (2023)](https://www.deseret.com/23581348/lucky-girl-syndrome-tiktok/); [Canvas8 (2023)](https://www.canvas8.com/blog/2023/july/why-is-lucky-girl-syndrome-taking-over-tiktok); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/lucky-girl-syndrome-tiktok-manifestation-positive-thinking-1774608)
- March 2025: per Google Trends, manifestation "really spiked in 2020" and is "still riding that wave" — [NPR, Mar 2025](https://www.npr.org/2025/03/31/1263527067/manifestation-internet-lucky-girl-syndrome)
- "Manifestation journal" search index 73 in Oct 2025 and 64 in Dec 2025 (steady interest) — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/manifesting_trend_tiktok) (third-party)
- Subliminal audiences: comment sections indicate mostly Gen Z, creators mostly young women — [Snake Oil substack](https://snakeoildotbiz.substack.com/p/what-the-hell-are-internet-subliminals); [New Statesman, Nov 2025](https://www.newstatesman.com/culture/life/2025/11/youtube-subliminals-want-to-make-you-perfect)
- International: top channels include a German creator (Akuo) — [ytshark](https://ytshark.com/?p=166); multi-language slideshow accounts are used for spiritual apps — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/tiktok-multi-account-slideshow-strategy-making-50m-views-per-month/)

### Inferences
- Target users: women 16–28, US first, then UK, Canada, Australia; worth trying Spanish/Portuguese/German content later. Because many are teens, use App Store age rating and pricing carefully (cheaper weekly or annual plans; parents may control payments).
- The trend is mature rather than peaking, so expect steady demand, not a viral wave by default.

### Gaps
- No 2026 Google Trends data for "subliminal", "Neville Goddard", "369 method" found; run Google Trends directly.
- No reliable gender/age/country split for subliminal YouTube audiences.

## 4. Monetization benchmarks (health, wellness, self-care subscriptions)

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness is the category where annual plans win: median roughly $40/year, $9.99/month, $4.99/week, trial-to-paid ~35–38%, and 5–9 day trials are the norm. Hard paywalls in onboarding convert much better than freemium, and annual subscribers retain far better than weekly.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat 2026, Health & Fitness medians: download-to-trial 6.9% (top >23%); download-to-paid 2.9% (top quartile >6.2%); trial-to-paid 37.7% (top quartile >51.4%); 82.1% of trials start on Day 0 — [RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- RevenueCat 2026 H&F prices: weekly $4.99, monthly $9.99, yearly ~$39.94; annual plans are 68% of subscriptions sold (highest category); weekly and lifetime have the lowest shares of any category; 5–9 day trials used by 54%; 60% of paywalls show 2 plans — [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- RevenueCat 2026 H&F revenue per install: $0.48 at Day 14 (highest category), $0.66 at Day 60; realized LTV per payer $24.23 after 1 month, $35.64 after 1 year — [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/); [Mobile Marketing Reads](https://www.mobilemarketingreads.com/?p=50360)
- RevenueCat 2026 cross-category: download-to-paid 10.7% for hard paywalls vs 2.1% for freemium — [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/). Trials of 17–32 days converted at 42.5% vs 25.5% for ≤4 days — [SaaStr summary](https://www.saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps/)
- RevenueCat 2025: H&F median trial-to-paid 39.9%, top 10% 68.3%; iOS converts about 2x Android — [Athletech News (2025)](https://athletechnews.com/?p=124212)
- Adapty 2026: H&F trial-to-paid 35.0% (highest category); annual plans 60.6% of H&F revenue; H&F first-renewal retention 30.3% (lowest category) — [Adapty State of In-App Subscriptions 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2026)
- Adapty 2026 cross-category: median prices $7.48/week, $12.99/month, $38.42/year; install-to-trial 10.9%; weekly plans 55.5% of all app revenue (up from 43.3% in 2023); onboarding paywalls without trials convert at 37.45% but give the lowest long-term value; hard paywalls give 21% higher LTV per subscriber than soft; Day-380 retention: annual 19.9%, monthly 14.2%, weekly 5.5%; European apps charge 29–39% more than North American — [Adapty 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2026)
- Revenue is concentrated: top 10 apps take most category revenue — [PPC Land on Adapty 2026](https://ppc.land/95-of-app-subscription-revenue-goes-to-top-10-adaptys-2026-benchmark-report/)
- Niche reference: I am uses a quiz-style onboarding ending in a 3-day trial into an annual plan — [Screens Design](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/i-am-daily-affirmations)

### Inferences
- Starting point: onboarding quiz (goals: face, money, confidence, sleep) leading to a hard paywall with two plans, annual ~$29.99–39.99 with a 7-day trial (default) and weekly ~$4.99 without trial; consider a lifetime offer (~$59–79) only as a paywall-exit or later promo, since it is a small share in this category.
- Free tier option: let users listen to a few built-in subliminals free (for TikTok-driven installs who want to try), gate "make your own"/voice/custom affirmations behind the paywall.
- At median H&F numbers, each install is worth about $0.50 in two weeks, so any paid channel with CPI above ~$1 needs top-quartile conversion to pay back.

### Gaps
- No benchmarks specifically for affirmation/subliminal apps (refund rates, teen payment rates).
- Superwall paywall test data not reviewed.

## 5. Store policy risks

### Takeaway
The main risks are making health or medical claims (Apple 1.4.1, 1.1.6), misleading marketing (2.3.1), the "saturated category" spam rule (4.3, which names fortune telling), and AI/voice data rules (Apple 5.1.2(i) third-party AI consent; Google Play AI-Generated Content and July 2026 User Data update). Neither store has an explicit "subliminal" or "voice cloning consent" rule that I could find.

### Cited Findings
- Apple 1.4.1: medical apps that could give inaccurate information or be used for diagnosis/treatment get greater scrutiny; accuracy claims must be backed by disclosed data and methodology or the app is rejected; apps should remind users to check with a doctor — [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Developers report 1.4.1 rejections even for wellness-only apps — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/807508); [forum thread](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/772732)
- Apple 1.1.6 (false information): labelling an app "for entertainment purposes" won't overcome the guideline — [Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Apple 2.3.1: misleading marketing, inside or outside the App Store, is grounds for removal and account termination — [Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Apple 4.3(b): saturated categories (dating, flashlight, sound effects, wallpaper, simple timers, fortune telling) won't accept new apps unless meaningfully different — [Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Apple 5.1.2(i): must disclose where personal data is shared with third parties "including with third-party AI" and get explicit permission first — [Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (updated Nov 2025 per [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/fr/article/news-apple-app-review-guidelines-ai-data-sharing/))
- Apple 1.2 lists AI content creation apps among those needing moderation safeguards; 2.3.9 requires rights to all materials in screenshots/previews — [Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Google Play July 15, 2026 announcement: User Data policy applies to third-party AI integrations; developers responsible for limited use, disclosure and consent; at least 30 days to comply — [Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17134731?hl=en)
- Google Play AI-Generated Content policy covers content from text, voice and image prompts; prohibits voice or video recordings of real people that facilitate scams; requires safeguards against offensive output — [Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14094294?hl=en)
- Google Aug 2026: policies prohibit creating/distributing non-consensual sexual content via generative AI — [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/08/ensuring-safety-genai-preventing-non-consensual-intimate-content.html)
- Voice-cloning vendors (not stores) require the speaker to read a consent statement before a voice model is made — [Vorec](https://vorec.ai/blog/ai-voice-cloning)

### Inferences
- Don't claim physical changes ("change your face", "grow hair", "heal") in store copy or screenshots; frame as affirmations, mindset and listening experience. Creator-made TikToks claiming "results" could count as the app's own misleading marketing under 2.3.1 if run as paid/affiliate content.
- Only clone the user's own voice, record a spoken consent line, and disclose the voice provider (e.g. ElevenLabs) in-app and in the privacy policy before upload, to satisfy Apple 5.1.2(i) and Google's User Data rules.
- Present the app as distinct from generic "affirmation player" clones (custom subliminal maker, own voice) to avoid 4.3.

### Gaps
- No official Apple/Google text naming "subliminal" content.
- Remaining part of Apple guidelines (after 5.2.5) not read; FTC rules on influencer disclosure and health claims not researched.
